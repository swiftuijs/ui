## Usage and limitations

- Expands horizontally in HStack and vertically in VStack. Multiple spacers share the remaining space equally.
- `minLength` sets a minimum; the parent needs available space, supplied by explicit dimensions or its layout.
- Use it for flexible separation, and check narrow containers so minimum lengths do not crowd content.
