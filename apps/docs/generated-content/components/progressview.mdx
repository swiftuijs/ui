---
title: "ProgressView"
description: "Display measurable progress or an indeterminate task."
---

## Usage and limitations

- Use `value` plus `total` for real units. Without `total`, `value` is a fraction from 0 to 1. Legacy `progress` and `completed` aliases remain supported.
- `indeterminate` removes numeric value semantics and animates when duration is unknown.
- `label` and `currentValueLabel` display task/progress text; keep them in sync with the actual work. Provide visible or accessible labeling and place progress near its task.
